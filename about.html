What is CatchTheBrief?

CatchTheBrief is a daily briefing covering India's tech and startup ecosystem. Every morning, we publish 5 sharp, story-driven summaries of the most important news — written in plain English, not press release language.

No paywalls. No spam. No fluff. Just the stories that matter to founders, engineers, product managers, and investors building in India.

How it works

Each night, we scan India's leading tech publications — YourStory, Inc42, Entrackr, The Ken, MediaNama, TechCrunch India, Gadgets 360, and more. The most impactful stories are selected, rewritten for clarity, and published by 8 AM IST.

Every brief includes the hook, the backstory, the key numbers, what happens next, and why it matters specifically for India. Plus an editorial take — our honest opinion on what it all means.

Who makes this?

CatchTheBrief is an independent project, built and curated in India. It runs entirely on AI-assisted tooling and GitHub Actions — zero servers, zero paywalls.
