Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

What we collect

When you subscribe to the CatchTheBrief newsletter, we collect your email address. This is processed by MailerLite, our email service provider. We do not collect names, phone numbers, or any other personal data.

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic — page views, referral sources, and general geography. No personally identifiable information is stored by us.

How we use it

Your email is used solely to send you the daily CatchTheBrief newsletter. We will never sell, rent, or share your email with third parties. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.

Cookies

Google Analytics uses cookies to measure site usage. You can opt out using Google's opt-out tool.

Third-party services

We use the following services: MailerLite (email), Google Analytics (traffic), and GitHub Pages (hosting). Each has its own privacy policy.
